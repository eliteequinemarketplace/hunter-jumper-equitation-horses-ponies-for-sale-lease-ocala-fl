Ocala, Florida is one of the most important equestrian centers in the world — home to more horses per capita than anywhere else in the United States, with over 70,000 horses and 1,200 horse farms spread across Marion County's rolling countryside. For buyers searching for hunter jumper horses for sale in Ocala, FL, Elite Equine Marketplace connects you directly with sellers in the heart of Florida's year-round horse country.

HITS Ocala — Year-Round Competition

HITS Ocala hosts major hunter jumper competitions throughout the year, providing Ocala-based horses with consistent show experience and verified competition records. Horses competing at HITS Ocala have access to world-class footing, facilities, and competition — and buyers can observe horses competing live before arranging a trial. Elite Equine Marketplace lists horses from HITS Ocala competitors and Ocala-based trainers year-round.

World Equestrian Center — Ocala

The World Equestrian Center (WEC) in Ocala opened in 2021 and has rapidly become one of the premier equestrian facilities in the country — hosting major hunter jumper competitions, FEI events, and the WEC Premier Equitation Cup Championship throughout the year. WEC Ocala also hosts major USHJA International Hunter Derby classes, making it a key venue for derby horse buyers and sellers in the Florida market.

Ocala's Horse Farm Community

Ocala's extensive horse farm community includes breeding farms, training facilities, and sale barns representing every type of hunter jumper horse — from green warmblood imports just arriving from Europe through seasoned show horses with years of USEF competition records. The combination of year-round training climate, world-class competition venues, and a deep concentration of equestrian expertise makes Ocala one of the best places in the world to buy or sell a hunter jumper horse.
